## 1. Purpose and identity
NIKWAY is an ecosystem for understanding, learning, capability development, and continuous improvement in economic enterprises. It connects people, knowledge, processes, technology and evidence. This identity is grounded in the repository README; the agentic third-order knowledge architecture below is a **new hypothesis**, not an already verified product capability.

## 2. Seed proposition
No stored assertion becomes established truth merely by being recorded, repeated, generated by an agent, or approved by an authority. Every consequential claim must be distinguishable from its evidence, source, context, interpretation and authorization.

## 3. Distinct registers
- **Source of record:** canonical location of a particular record.
- **Source of evidence:** material supporting or challenging a claim.
- **Authority:** who may approve a policy, definition or change.
- **Epistemic status:** proposed, observed, inferred, corroborated, disputed, or superseded.
- **Approval status:** draft, approved, rejected or withdrawn, tracked separately from epistemic status.

## 4. Three orders — design hypothesis
1. First order: model the domain, reason about problems and act within authority.
2. Second order: inspect sources, uncertainty, reasoning and outcomes.
3. Third order: evaluate and propose revisions to the methods for knowledge creation, validation, learning and decision-making, subject to governed review.
This is a NIKWAY operational definition, **not** a universally accepted taxonomy of third-order systems.

## 5. Foundational concepts
Identity; phenomenon; distinction; context; concept; relation; claim; evidence; provenance; rule; policy; knowledge; skill; method; tool; agent; action; outcome; reflection; revision.
Definitions, identifiers and relationships require a separate versioned ontology; this list does not constitute one.

## 6. Memory and authority
Memory ownership scopes: individual, team, organizational, institutional, federated. These are governance scopes, not cognitive memory types or database prescriptions.
Each knowledge record should carry an identifier, source, context, timestamps, version, epistemic status, approval status, owner and retention policy.
Domain systems remain authoritative for their own operational records; Git is authoritative only for this versioned architectural proposal.

## 7. Lifecycle
Observe → interpret → register → validate → classify → retrieve → apply → measure → reflect → propose revision → review/approve → version/retain/retire.
Agent proposals never bypass deterministic permissions, human accountability or evidence gates.

## 8. Methodological references (to verify and map)

- W3C PROV-O — entity, activity, agent and provenance.
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 — architecture description and viewpoints.
- Popper — fallibility and critical testing of empirical claims.

- BABOK, BIZBOK, EFQM, Baldrige and Shingo — candidate method and competency libraries, not foundational axioms.

## 9. Initial acceptance tests
- Given a claim with no evidence, system must not label it verified.
- Given conflicting claims, preserve both provenance chains and flag conflict.
- Given a changed organizational policy, retain prior version and approval history.
- Given an agent-proposed methodological change, require independent evaluation and authorized approval before activation.
- Given cross-tenant retrieval, deny unauthorized disclosure.
